# 15-minute diagnostic

Fifteen original items, three from each skill area, to show you where to start. Set a timer for 15 minutes, use paper and a pencil but no calculator, answer everything, then check your answers and read the table at the bottom.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from any local's test, publisher or prep site.

If you already know which skills your local tests and want a full picture of your pacing, take the [free timed pipe trades practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pipefitter-aptitude?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pipe-trades-aptitude-test) instead.

## Part A: Arithmetic, fractions and measurement

**D1.** 2 5/8 + 1 3/4 = ?

A. 4 3/8 · B. 3 8/12 · C. 4 1/8 · D. 3 7/8

**D2.** How many inches are there in 1 ft 9 in?

A. 19 · B. 109 · C. 27 · D. 21

**D3.** What is 30% of 250?

A. 25 · B. 83 · C. 75 · D. 220

## Part B: Algebra and formulas

**D4.** Solve 6x − 5 = 31.

A. 4 · B. 5 · C. 6 · D. 36

**D5.** Voltage V = I × R. What is V when I = 4 and R = 6?

A. 10 · B. 1.5 · C. 24 · D. 2

**D6.** Solve 3(x + 2) = 2x + 11.

A. 5 · B. 3 · C. 9 · D. 13

## Part C: Reading comprehension

> Cross-linked polyethylene tubing, usually called PEX, is flexible enough to bend around gentle corners. That means a PEX run often needs fewer fittings than the same run in rigid pipe, and fewer fittings means fewer places that can leak. PEX should not be left in direct sunlight for long periods, because ultraviolet light weakens it. For this reason it is stored indoors, or kept in its original covering, until it is installed.

**D7.** According to the passage, why does a PEX run often need fewer fittings?

A. It is cheaper than rigid pipe. · B. It does not leak. · C. It can bend around gentle corners. · D. It is stored indoors.

**D8.** Why is PEX kept indoors or covered before installation?

A. To keep it clean · B. To stop it from bending · C. Because sunlight weakens it · D. Because it is easily stolen

**D9.** Which is the best title for the passage?

A. How to Install PEX · B. The History of Plastic Pipe · C. Why Rigid Pipe Leaks · D. PEX: Fewer Fittings, but Keep It Out of the Sun

## Part D: Mechanical reasoning

Ignore friction and the weight of levers and pulleys.

**D10.** A 15-tooth gear turning at 90 rpm drives a 45-tooth gear. How fast does the 45-tooth gear turn?

A. 15 rpm · B. 30 rpm · C. 45 rpm · D. 270 rpm

**D11.** Four gears sit in a row, each meshing with the next. The first gear turns clockwise. Which way does the fourth gear turn?

A. Clockwise · B. Counterclockwise · C. It cannot turn

**D12.** A 60 pound load sits 2 feet from the pivot of a lever. How much downward force, 4 feet from the pivot on the other side, balances it?

A. 15 lb · B. 120 lb · C. 60 lb · D. 30 lb

## Part E: Spatial reasoning

**D13.** A cube net is a vertical column of four squares labelled, from top to bottom, P, Q, S and U, with square M attached to the left of Q and square N attached to the right of Q. When it is folded into a cube, which face is opposite P?

A. Q · B. S · C. U · D. N

**D14.** A large cube is made of 8 small cubes, two along each edge. The whole outside is painted. How many small cubes have paint on exactly three faces?

A. 0 · B. 4 · C. 6 · D. 8

**D15.** A sheet of paper is folded in half once. One hole is punched through both layers. How many holes are there when it is unfolded?

A. 1 · B. 2 · C. 3 · D. 4

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations</summary>

**D1: A, 4 3/8.** Whole numbers: 2 + 1 = 3. Fractions: 5/8 + 3/4 = 5/8 + 6/8 = 11/8 = 1 3/8. Total 4 3/8. *Why not B:* 8/12 comes from adding tops and bottoms.

**D2: D, 21.** 1 ft = 12 in, so 12 + 9 = 21.

**D3: C, 75.** 10% of 250 is 25, so 30% is 75.

**D4: C, 6.** Add 5: 6x = 36. Divide by 6: x = 6. *Why not D:* 36 is 6x, not x.

**D5: C, 24.** 4 × 6 = 24. *Why not A:* 10 adds instead of multiplying.

**D6: A, 5.** Expand: 3x + 6 = 2x + 11. Subtract 2x: x + 6 = 11. Subtract 6: x = 5. Check: 3 × 7 = 21 and 10 + 11 = 21.

**D7: C.** It is "flexible enough to bend around gentle corners". *Why not B:* the passage says fewer places that can leak, not that it never leaks.

**D8: C.** "Ultraviolet light weakens it."

**D9: D.** The passage covers two points: fewer fittings and sun damage. D is the only title that covers both.

**D10: B, 30 rpm.** 15 × 90 = 1,350, and 1,350 ÷ 45 = 30. The bigger gear turns more slowly.

**D11: B, counterclockwise.** Gears 1 and 3 turn clockwise; gears 2 and 4 turn counterclockwise.

**D12: D, 30 lb.** 60 × 2 = 120, and 120 ÷ 4 = 30.

**D13: B, S.** P and S have Q between them in the column, so they are opposite. Q is opposite U, and M is opposite N.

**D14: D, 8.** In a 2 by 2 by 2 cube every small cube is a corner, so every one has three painted faces.

**D15: B, 2.** One fold makes two layers, so one punch makes two holes.

</details>
