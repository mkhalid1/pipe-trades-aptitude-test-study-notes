# Which test does my local use?

This is the most important page in the repo, because the answer decides what you study. The United Association does not run one national entrance test. Each local's joint apprenticeship committee chooses its own, and they choose very differently. Below is how to find out what yours uses, followed by real examples taken from locals' own postings and from state recruitment notices.

## How to find out in four steps

1. **Find your local.** Use the "Find a Local Union" search on the [UA website](https://ua.org/career-paths/apprentice/). Plumbers, pipefitters, steamfitters and sprinkler fitters may be in different locals in the same city, so check which one covers the trade you want.
2. **Read the local's apprenticeship or "apply" page.** Most name the test, the order of steps and any documents you need. Some also post a study guide or sample questions.
3. **Check the state notice.** In some states, such as New York, the Department of Labor publishes each registered program's recruitment notice, and those notices often state the test and the qualifying score.
4. **Call the training center** if the test still is not named. Ask four questions: which test, how long, is a calculator allowed, and is there a study guide.

Write the answers down. Then use the table in [what each test means for your study](#what-each-test-means-for-your-study) to decide which section guides to focus on.

## What each test means for your study

| If your local uses | Focus on these guides |
|---|---|
| Its own math exam | [Arithmetic, fractions and measurement](sections/arithmetic-fractions-and-measurement.md) first, then [algebra](sections/algebra-and-formulas.md) |
| WorkKeys | [Arithmetic](sections/arithmetic-fractions-and-measurement.md), [reading](sections/reading-comprehension.md), and reading values from drawings in [spatial reasoning](sections/spatial-reasoning.md) |
| Wonderlic Basic Skills or Accuplacer | [Arithmetic](sections/arithmetic-fractions-and-measurement.md), [algebra](sections/algebra-and-formulas.md) and [reading](sections/reading-comprehension.md) |
| Bennett Mechanical Comprehension | [Mechanical reasoning](sections/mechanical-reasoning.md) |
| GAN, DAT, Ramsay or a mixed battery | All five guides, with extra time on [mechanical](sections/mechanical-reasoning.md) and [spatial](sections/spatial-reasoning.md) |
| Math plus mechanical | [Arithmetic](sections/arithmetic-fractions-and-measurement.md), [algebra](sections/algebra-and-formulas.md) and [mechanical](sections/mechanical-reasoning.md) |
| Not named yet | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md) and start with your weakest area. Fractions and measurement are the safest first bet: they appear in almost every test above. |

## Claims you will see online

Prep sites often say "the UA test" has a fixed number of questions, a fixed time limit or a 70% pass mark everywhere. We could not find any UA or local page that says so for all locals. Some locals do use 70%, some use 75%, some use WorkKeys levels, and many do not publish a cut-off at all. Trust your own local's page over any general claim, including the examples on this one.

## Practise once you know

When you know which skills your test covers, try the [free timed pipe trades practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pipefitter-aptitude?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pipe-trades-aptitude-test). It mixes math, reading, mechanical and spatial items, so you can see which parts of a battery slow you down.
