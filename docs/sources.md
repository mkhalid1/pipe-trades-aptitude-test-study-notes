# Sources

Every fact in these notes about how locals test comes from the UA, from a local's own posting or training center page, or from a state recruitment notice. Each source below was checked live on 24 November 2026. Locals change their process often, so if something has changed, please [open an issue](https://github.com/mkhalid1/pipe-trades-aptitude-test-study-notes/issues).

## What we could not confirm

Prep sites and older summaries make claims we could not find on any local's own page, so they are not used as facts here:

- that one standard "UA aptitude test" exists for all locals
- a fixed format such as "140 questions in 120 minutes"
- a universal 70% or 75% pass mark
- the specific test used by several locals that are often named online, where we found no current page from the local itself

## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not statements by the UA or any local, and are labelled as guidance where they appear:

- the per-item timing advice in each section guide
- the "around 85% on timed practice" target
- the methods, traps lists, study plans and checklist
- all practice items and explanations, which are original
