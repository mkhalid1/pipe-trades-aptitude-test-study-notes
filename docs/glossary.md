# Glossary

Terms you will meet in locals' notices, on the test and in these notes.

## Apprenticeship and selection

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **UA** | The United Association of Union Plumbers, Fitters, Welders and Service Techs, the union for the pipe trades in the US and Canada. |
| **Local** | A local union of the UA, covering a set area and often specific trades. |
| **JAC / JATC** | Joint Apprenticeship Committee or Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee: union and contractor representatives who run a local's apprenticeship, including recruitment and testing. |
| **Training center** | The school a local's apprentices attend. It often handles applications and testing. |
| **Recruitment window** | The dates during which a local accepts applications. Some locals open once a year. |
| **Ranking list** | The ordered list of qualified applicants. Apprentices are usually taken from the top. Also called a waitlist or eligibility list. |
| **Signatory contractor** | A contractor that has signed an agreement with the local and employs its apprentices. |
| **Registered apprenticeship** | A program registered with the state or federal government, which sets standards for recruitment and training. |

## Tests

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Aptitude test** | A test of how well you are likely to learn a skill, rather than what you already know. |
| **WorkKeys** | A set of workplace skills tests from ACT. Locals name specific ones, such as Applied Math, Workplace Documents and Graphic Literacy. |
| **Accuplacer** | A placement test series. Steamfitters Local 601 names its Arithmetic, Algebra (QRAS) and Reading tests. |
| **Wonderlic Basic Skills** | A basic skills test that Local 7 uses. |
| **Bennett Mechanical Comprehension Test** | A picture-based mechanical reasoning test that Local 81 names. |
| **GAN aptitude test** | A trades aptitude battery that Steamfitters Local 601 names. |
| **DAT** | The Differential Aptitude Test, a battery that Local 189 may use. |
| **Ramsay test** | A family of mechanical and maintenance aptitude tests; Local 189 may use one. |

## Math and measurement

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Numerator / denominator** | The top and bottom numbers of a fraction. In 3/8, 3 is the numerator and 8 the denominator. |
| **Common denominator** | A shared bottom number that lets you add or subtract fractions. |
| **Mixed number** | A whole number and a fraction together, such as 2 1/2. |
| **Improper fraction** | A fraction larger than 1, such as 5/2. |
| **Reciprocal** | A fraction turned upside down. Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal. |
| **Offset** | A change in a pipe's line to get around an obstacle. |
| **Travel** | The length along the angled piece of an offset. For a 45 degree offset, travel = offset × 1.414. |
| **Run / rise** | The horizontal and vertical distances a pipe covers. |
| **Scale** | The ratio between a drawing and real size, such as 1/4 inch = 1 foot. |

## Mechanical and spatial

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **rpm** | Revolutions per minute: how fast something turns. |
| **Idler gear** | A gear between two others that changes direction but not the overall speed ratio. |
| **Pivot / fulcrum** | The point a lever turns on. |
| **Mechanical advantage** | How much a machine multiplies your force, paid for by moving a longer distance. |
| **Movable pulley** | A pulley that moves with the load and halves the force needed. |
| **Net** | A flat pattern that folds into a 3D shape. |
| **Isometric drawing** | A 3D-looking drawing with all three axes at equal angles, used for pipe drawings. |
| **Water hammer** | A pressure spike caused when moving water is stopped suddenly. |
