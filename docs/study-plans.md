# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when your test date is only a couple of days away. Both assume you have already found out [which test your local uses](which-test-does-my-local-use.md) and taken the [15-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Expect about 45 to 90 minutes a day on the 7-day plan.

If your local's test covers only some skills, for example a math-only exam, skip the days for the skills it does not test and give that time to your weakest tested skill.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the [test overview](test-overview.md) and confirm your local's test. Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Note your two weakest parts. |
| 2 | Fractions and measurement | Read [arithmetic, fractions and measurement](sections/arithmetic-fractions-and-measurement.md). Do all 12 items with no calculator. Then write 10 fraction sums of your own, mixing eighths and sixteenths, and solve them. |
| 3 | Algebra and formulas | Read [algebra and formulas](sections/algebra-and-formulas.md). Do all 12 items. Check every answer by substituting it back. |
| 4 | Reading | Read [reading comprehension](sections/reading-comprehension.md). Do all 12 items, proving each answer with a sentence from the passage. |
| 5 | Mechanical and spatial | Read [mechanical reasoning](sections/mechanical-reasoning.md) and [spatial reasoning](sections/spatial-reasoning.md). Learn the mechanical rules table. Fold a paper cube from a cross-shaped net. |
| 6 | First timed run | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pipefitter-aptitude?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pipe-trades-aptitude-test). Score it by skill. Review every miss with the three questions below. |
| 7 | Fix the weakest skill, then rest | Redo the items you missed this week without looking at the answers. Do 20 fresh items in your weakest skill. Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

For each miss, answer three questions in one line each:

1. **What went wrong?** For example: added the denominators, forgot to borrow 12 inches, counted gears wrong, chose an answer the passage does not state.
2. **What should have stopped me?** The word, number or rule that was the clue.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "convert to inches first" or "sketch the gears".

Keep these in one list. By the end of the week the same two or three mistakes will keep showing up. Fixing those is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when the test date is close. Many locals test only once or twice a year, so make this attempt count.

### Day 1: learn the rules

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 30 min | Read the "method" and "traps" parts of the section guides for every skill your local tests. Skip the rest for now. |
| 3 | 45 min | Do the practice items for your two weakest skills. Read every explanation, including the ones you got right. |
| 4 | 45 min | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pipefitter-aptitude?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pipe-trades-aptitude-test). |
| 5 | 20 min | Review every miss with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: sharpen and sit the test

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 min | Redo yesterday's misses without the answers. |
| 2 | 15 min | Warm up with 10 fraction and feet-and-inches items. These appear on almost every test and reward a warm brain. |
| 3 | 10 min | Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Pack what you need the night before. |
| 4 | Test | Sit the test rested and fed. Work steadily and write your steps down. |

If you only have one evening, do day 1 blocks 1, 2 and 4, then get a full night's sleep.

## What not to do

- **Do not study for a test your local does not use.** A week of gear problems will not help on a math-only exam. Check first.
- **Do not practise with a calculator** unless your local says one is allowed. Many do not allow them, and UA Local 5 says so directly.
- **Do not cram the night before.** Tired test takers drop signs and misread units, which is where most marks go on these tests.
- **Do not hunt for "real" questions.** Locals and testing companies change their forms, and passing round real items can break the test rules. Learn the skills instead.
