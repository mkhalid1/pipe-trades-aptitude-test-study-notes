# Test-day checklist

Most pipe trades entrance tests are taken in person, at the local's training center or at a testing center the local names. The San Jose training center, for example, says its exam "is administered in person at the Training Center", and Local 43's WorkKeys test is taken at Chattanooga State. The details below are general good practice. Where your local's letter or page says something different, follow the local.

## The day before

- [ ] Re-read your test letter or email: date, start time, address and what to bring.
- [ ] Check whether a calculator is allowed. If the letter does not say, plan to work without one.
- [ ] Gather any documents the local asked for. Many ask for photo ID; some ask for other papers at the test.
- [ ] Do a short warm-up only: 10 fraction items and a few gear items. No full practice test.
- [ ] Plan the trip and allow extra time for traffic and parking.
- [ ] Sleep. A rested brain makes fewer sign and unit slips.

## The morning of the test

- [ ] Eat a proper meal. Some sittings are long: the San Jose exam allows three and a half hours.
- [ ] Bring two sharpened pencils and an eraser unless the letter says materials are provided.
- [ ] Bring a watch without a calculator, if watches are allowed, so you can pace yourself.
- [ ] Arrive early. Late arrivals may not be let in.

## In the first minute

- [ ] Listen to or read the instructions fully. Note the time limit for each part, if there are parts.
- [ ] Check whether scrap paper is allowed and use it for every calculation.
- [ ] Look at how many items there are and set a rough pace: time allowed divided by items.

## During the test

- [ ] Write your steps down for every math item.
- [ ] For fractions, find a common denominator before you add or subtract.
- [ ] For feet and inches, work in inches, then convert back.
- [ ] Sketch every gear, lever, net and fold.
- [ ] In reading items, prove each answer with a sentence from the passage.
- [ ] If an item is taking too long, mark it, guess, and move on. Come back if there is time.
- [ ] Answer every item unless the instructions mention a penalty for wrong answers.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Write down which skill areas felt hardest while you still remember. If you need to test again, that list is your study plan.
- [ ] Note when and how the local said it would tell you the result.
- [ ] If you qualify for an interview, prepare for it as seriously as the test. Your overall rank can depend on both.

## Last practice before the real one

If you have a day or two left, take one timed run on the [free pipe trades practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pipefitter-aptitude?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=anh84w&utm_content=pipe-trades-aptitude-test), then rest.
