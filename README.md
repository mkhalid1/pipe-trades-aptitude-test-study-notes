# Pipe Trades Aptitude Test Study Notes

A free, complete study guide for the entrance test you take to join a UA plumbing, pipefitting, steamfitting or HVACR apprenticeship. There is no single "UA test": each local chooses its own, from a local math exam to WorkKeys, Wonderlic, Bennett, Accuplacer or an aptitude battery. These notes show you how to find out which one your local uses, then teach the five skills those tests share: arithmetic with fractions and measurements, algebra, reading, mechanical reasoning and spatial reasoning. You get a clear method for each, 75 original practice items with full explanations, what locals actually publish about scoring, and study plans for a week or a weekend. Facts about testing come only from the UA, locals' own pages and state recruitment notices, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Take the [free timed pipe trades practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pipefitter-aptitude?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pipe-trades-aptitude-test), or the shorter [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md) in this repo.

## Start here

1. **Find out which test your local uses.** Follow the four steps on [which test does my local use](docs/which-test-does-my-local-use.md). It also shows real examples from 15 locals.
2. **Find your weak skills.** Take the [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md). It has three items from each skill area and tells you where to start.
3. **Fix the weakest skill first.** Open its guide below, learn the method, then do its 12 practice items.
4. **Follow a plan.** Use the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan), or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if your test date is close.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Who sets the test | Each local's joint apprenticeship committee (JAC or JATC), not the UA nationally |
| Trades | Plumber, pipefitter, steamfitter, welder, HVACR and service tech, depending on the local |
| Tests locals name | A local math exam, ACT WorkKeys, Wonderlic Basic Skills, Bennett Mechanical Comprehension, Accuplacer, the GAN aptitude test, the DAT or a Ramsay test |
| Skills tested | Fractions and measurement, algebra, reading, mechanical reasoning, spatial reasoning |
| Calculator | Often not allowed. Check your local. |
| Pass mark | No national one. Some locals publish theirs, such as 70% or 75%. [Examples](docs/scoring.md#pass-marks-that-locals-publish) |
| After the test | Usually an interview and a ranking list. Scores often set your rank. |
| Trade knowledge | Not asked for in any published test description we found |

Sources: [UA apprenticeship page](https://ua.org/career-paths/apprentice/), locals' own postings and New York State Department of Labor notices, all listed in [sources](docs/sources.md) and checked 24 November 2026.

## The five skill areas at a glance

| Skill area | What you do | Where it shows up | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic, fractions and measurement | Fractions, decimals, percentages, feet and inches, tape reading | Almost every test | [Arithmetic](docs/sections/arithmetic-fractions-and-measurement.md) |
| Algebra and formulas | Solve equations, use and rearrange formulas | Accuplacer, many local math exams | [Algebra](docs/sections/algebra-and-formulas.md) |
| Reading comprehension | Answer questions on technical passages and procedures | WorkKeys, Accuplacer, batteries | [Reading](docs/sections/reading-comprehension.md) |
| Mechanical reasoning | Gears, levers, pulleys, pressure and flow | Bennett, batteries, math plus mechanical exams | [Mechanical](docs/sections/mechanical-reasoning.md) |
| Spatial reasoning | Folding, rotating, counting blocks, views | Aptitude batteries such as GAN and DAT | [Spatial](docs/sections/spatial-reasoning.md) |

Every guide has the same parts: what it measures, how the questions look, a step-by-step method, the traps that cost points, timing advice, 12 original practice items with explanations, and a score table.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **Start with your local.** Only study what your local's test covers. A math-only exam needs no gear practice.
- **Work without a calculator,** unless your local says one is allowed. Use paper and a pencil.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Answers are hidden under "Answers and explanations". Commit to an answer before you open them.
- **Read every explanation,** including for items you got right. Most say why the most tempting wrong answer is wrong.
- **Keep a miss log.** For each miss, write what went wrong. The [study plans](docs/study-plans.md#how-to-review-a-missed-item) show how.
- **Practise timed.** The notes teach the methods. A timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/pipefitter-aptitude?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=pipe-trades-aptitude-test), teaches the pace.
- **Clone or fork it** if you want your own annotated copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

## About the practice items

All 75 practice items are original, written for these notes: 12 in each of the five skill guides and 15 in the diagnostic. None are taken from any local's test, any publisher's materials or any prep site. Mechanical and spatial items are described fully in words so that every answer can be checked from the text.
